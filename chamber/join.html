Nonprofit Membership

Cost: Free

Benefits

  • Listing in the online business directory
  • Access to Chamber networking events
  • Volunteer and community partnership opportunities
  • Discounted rates for selected events

Bronze Membership

Cost: $100 per year

Benefits

  • Listing in the online business directory
  • Access to Chamber networking events
  • Business name included in the Chamber newsletter
  • Discounted admission to selected events

Silver Membership

Cost: $250 per year

Benefits

  • All Bronze Membership benefits
  • Rotating spotlight on the Chamber home page
  • Additional event discounts
  • Quarterly advertisement in the Chamber newsletter

Gold Membership

Cost: $500 per year

Benefits

  • All Silver Membership benefits
  • Priority spotlight placement on the home page
  • Featured advertising at Chamber events
  • Priority registration and larger event discounts
